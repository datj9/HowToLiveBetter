# High Cost-Performance Life Guide

Unofficial English translation of [eternity4719/HowToLiveBetter](https://github.com/eternity4719/HowToLiveBetter). Chinese original wins if anything conflicts.

How to live longer, get sick less, and handle emergencies. How to waste less money, avoid scams and legal traps. What to claim when you have no job or cash. What paperwork you need to open a shop, company, or site. Also love, marriage, children, going abroad, and skills.

About 641 tips. Each states cost, benefit, evidence grade (A/B/C), and sources limited to journal papers and official documents.

Not a checklist. Pick one or two items. The author has not done most of them.

## How to read

- Filter on the search page by keyword, chapter, evidence grade, and cost (money / time / willpower).
- Evidence: **A** meta-analysis or RCT / official rule; **B** single study; **C** experience or common practice.
- Cost-effectiveness is the author's ranking, not evidence. A high-evidence item can still be expensive.
- Keep sources and numbers as-is.
