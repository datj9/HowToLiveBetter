### 6. Do not feed honey to children under 1 year old
- Chi phí: No cost involved
- Nói thường: Never give honey to babies under 1 year of age. This includes honey water, honey mixed into baby food, and any baked goods containing honey. Honey can contain bacteria that cause botulism. An adult’s gut microbiome is able to keep these bacteria in check, but a baby’s cannot. The same rule applies to homemade pickled foods and fermented products.
- Lợi ích: According to the Chinese CDC, “Do not feed honey to children under 1 year of age” because “honey may contain bacteria that cause botulism.” An adult’s gut microbiome can suppress these bacterial spores — the dormant form of the bacteria — while a baby’s cannot.
- Bằng chứng: A
- Ghi chú: Foods and drinks to avoid include honey water, honey mixed into baby food, and any pastries containing honey. Likewise, homemade pickled foods and fermented products should not be given to infants.
- Nguồn: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
