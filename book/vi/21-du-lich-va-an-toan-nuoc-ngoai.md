Accepting such a job means you lose control over both yourself and your passport. Submitting false documents at the border will get you turned away. ### 8. Before driving abroad, verify whether local authorities recognize Chinese driver’s licenses; most “international driver’s permits” sold online are legally worthless.
- Chi phí: No cost at all. Simply check local regulations before you travel.
- Nói thường: Online services offering “international driver’s permits” hold no legal validity in mainland China. Such permits are merely translations recognized among signatory nations of the 1949 Geneva Convention on Road Traffic. Mainland China is not among the 103 signatory countries, so any permit issued domestically cannot be used legally. Whether a Chinese license is acceptable depends entirely on local laws — do not drive without confirming this first.
